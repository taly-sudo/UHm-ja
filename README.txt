gtra6 media gallery
===================

Open index.html in any browser (double-click it) to browse the gallery.

  index.html   the gallery page
  media.js     the list of items, captions and collections
  media/       copies of every photo and video, grouped by collection

The originals in the gtra6 folder were not modified or moved - everything
in media/ is a copy.

To regenerate captions or add media: drop new files into the matching
media/ subfolder and add an entry to media.js.
